Privacy Notice

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Notice describes how the Earth-Hub earthquake dashboard (the "Service"), available at earth-hub.diego-narvaez.workers.dev and operated by the author of the Earth-Hub project as an independent research project (the "Operator", "we", "us"), handles information about the persons who visit it ("you"). By using the Service, you acknowledge this Notice.

1. No collection of your location

The Service does not request, access, collect, record or store the geographic location of your device. It never invokes your browser's Geolocation API, and your browser will therefore never ask you for permission to share your location. The Operator does not record your IP address or any location derived from it, and the request logging that would record them is disabled. The locations shown on the map are those of earthquakes, published by the U.S. Geological Survey, and not those of visitors.

2. Information we do not collect

The Service does not:

  • require or offer accounts, registration, sign-in or forms of any kind;
  • set, read or use cookies;
  • store any information in your browser's local storage, session storage, IndexedDB or other site storage;
  • use analytics, advertising, social-media plug-ins, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or any other technology to identify, profile or track you;
  • sell, rent, share or otherwise disclose information about you to anyone.

3. Information processed by service providers

Delivering any website over the internet requires that your device's IP address and standard request information (such as the address requested, your browser's user-agent and the time of the request) be transmitted to the servers that deliver its content. The Service relies on the following independent providers, each of which processes such information under its own privacy policy and not on the Operator's instructions:

  • Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts the Service. Cloudflare processes your IP address and request information to deliver, protect and operate the Service, and may determine an approximate location from your IP address for routing and security purposes; it may also receive reports from your browser about failed connections (Network Error Logging). See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr, a public content-delivery network from which your browser loads the DuckDB database engine that the dashboard uses. See jsDelivr's terms and privacy policy.
  • OpenFreeMap, which serves the map tiles. Because tiles are requested for the area of the map you view, OpenFreeMap receives your IP address and the map areas requested. See OpenFreeMap.

The Operator does not receive, access or control the information these providers process, beyond the aggregate, non-identifying usage figures that a hosting provider reports to its customers.

4. Browser caching

Your browser may keep copies of the Service's files, its earthquake data and map tiles in its ordinary HTTP cache, as it does for any website, so that pages load faster. These copies contain no information about you and remain on your device under your control; you can remove them at any time by clearing your browser's cache.

5. The data the Service displays

The Service displays public earthquake data from the U.S. Geological Survey. That data contains no personal information about visitors to the Service.

6. Changes to this Notice

The Operator may update this Notice from time to time. Any change takes effect when the updated Notice is published on this page, as shown by its effective date. The history of every change is public in the project's source code.

7. Contact

Questions about this Notice may be raised by opening an issue on the Earth-Hub project's GitHub page.
